How exposure is counted

1. Rate each plant. Each hazard indicator is placed in one of five bands, low to extremely high, for today and for 2040.

2. Count revenue. Revenue at plants rated high or worse is exposed.

3. Follow the clinker. A grinding unit fed by an exposed kiln is exposed too, in proportion to the clinker it takes.

4. Show the spread. Climate figures are the middle of several models. Plant pages show the lowest and highest.

One climate pathway is available from the source, close to the high-emissions case. By 2040 the choice of pathway matters less than the spread between models, which is why the spread is shown.
